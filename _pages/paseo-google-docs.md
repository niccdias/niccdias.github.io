Paseo Google Docs lets my local Paseo agents work with my Google documents when I ask them to. It supports finding and reading documents, creating new documents, editing existing text, and adding or responding to comments, including inline comments on specific passages.

This is a personal connection maintained by [Nic Dias](/). It is used with my own Google account and is not a public service or an invitation to connect other accounts.

Google handles sign-in and permission approval. The connection uses Google's Docs and Drive APIs through the Google Workspace CLI on my computer. Google Drive access supports finding existing documents and working with their comments; Google Docs access supports reading and editing document content.

Agents may send relevant document content to the AI provider handling a requested task. The [privacy policy](/paseo-google-docs/privacy/) explains access, storage, sharing, and how to revoke the connection.

For questions, contact [niccdias@ps.au.dk](mailto:niccdias@ps.au.dk).
